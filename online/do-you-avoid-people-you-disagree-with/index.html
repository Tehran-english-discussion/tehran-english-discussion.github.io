Do You Avoid People You Disagree With?

Echo chambers, evidence, and how to disagree without turning a conversation into a competition.

Oct19
Monday, 19 Oct 202627 Mehr · 8–10 p.m. Tehran time
F
Moderated byFatemeh 💙

What the moderator asked

The questions

Borrow them for your own table.

  1. Do you avoid talking to people you disagree with? What determines whether you engage in a discussion or avoid it?
  2. Do we form our opinions based on evidence, or do we tend to look for evidence that supports what we already believe?
  3. Have you ever changed your mind about something important after listening to someone you disagreed with? What made you reconsider?
  4. How do social media and surrounding ourselves with like-minded people affect our ability to understand opposing viewpoints?
  5. Is avoiding a disagreement sometimes a healthy choice, or does it prevent us from growing? Where would you draw the line?
  6. What makes a disagreement productive rather than destructive, and how can we disagree without turning it into a competition?
