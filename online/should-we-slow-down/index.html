Should We Slow Down?

Hurry, boredom and the strange discomfort of having nothing to do.

Oct5
Monday, 5 Oct 202613 Mehr · 8–10 p.m. Tehran time
Moderated byNavid
See the next call →All past calls
What the moderator asked

The questions

Borrow them for your own table.

  1. How often do you feel that you are in a hurry, even when there is no real reason to be?
  2. Why do you think people often feel uncomfortable when they have “nothing to do”?
  3. When was the last time you deliberately slowed down and enjoyed doing something without worrying about the time?
  4. If you could remove one source of unnecessary hurry from your life, what would it be?
