Luck

Hard work or a lucky break? Chance, destiny, missed chances, and whether we make our own luck.

Nov30
Monday, 30 Nov 20269 Azar · 8–10 p.m. Tehran time
Your moderatorAmir 🏜
Take a seat →Add to my calendar ↓
Think about these before Monday

The questions

  1. How do you define luck? Do you consider yourself a lucky person?
  2. How much of our success comes from hard work, and how much comes from luck?
  3. Have you ever missed an opportunity that you later realized could have changed your life?
  4. Is luck something that happens to us, or something we can create?
  5. Why do some people seem to have all the luck while others constantly struggle?
  6. Can failure and bad luck actually be necessary ingredients for success?
  7. Do you believe in destiny, or do you think we have complete control over our lives?
  8. Imagine two people with exactly the same abilities, intelligence, and work ethic. One becomes extremely successful, while the other fails. What explains the difference?
  9. According to an ancient Chinese concept, luck is influenced by three factors: Heaven Luck, Earth Luck, and Human Luck. Do you think we can control our own luck, or are some things simply beyond our control?
  10. If you could go back in time and change one decision in your life, would you do it?
