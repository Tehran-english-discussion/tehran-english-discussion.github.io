Every topic we’ve talked about

Tehran English Discussion (.TED) is an English discussion club in Tehran. Every Friday, fluent speakers sit around one long café table and talk for two hours about life lessons, big ideas and the questions we can’t stop thinking about.

Since September 2025 there have been two tables each Friday, 4–6 p.m. and 6–8 p.m. Here is every session on record since Mar 2025, newest first, with the member who moderated it. For 56 of them you can open the questions the moderator brought; borrow them for your own table. We also meet online every other Monday.
