Before Friday

Know the legal terms

Parnian suggests learning these terms first. They may come up during the session, and knowing them will make the conversation easier.

!

Content warning. This session and the list below include violent and sexual crimes, which some people may find disturbing.

  1. MurderKilling someone on purpose, usually planned or intended.
  2. Serial murderOne person killing several people in separate events over time.
  3. Involuntary manslaughterCausing someone’s death without meaning to, through carelessness.
  4. Aggravated assaultA serious attack meant to cause severe injury, often with a weapon.
  5. BatteryPhysically touching or hitting someone in a harmful or unwanted way.
  6. Domestic violenceAbuse by a partner or family member inside the home.
  7. Sexual assaultAny sexual contact without consent.
  8. RapeForcing sexual intercourse on someone without consent.
  9. Sexual abuse of a minorAny sexual act with a child or someone under the legal age.
  10. Forced marriageA marriage that one or both people did not freely agree to.
  11. StalkingRepeatedly following, watching or contacting someone so that they feel afraid.
  12. KidnappingTaking and holding someone by force or by trickery.
  13. Human traffickingMoving or holding people by force or deceit to exploit them.
  14. TortureDeliberately causing someone severe pain or suffering.
  15. Child abductionTaking a child away from their parents or legal guardians.
  16. Armed robberyStealing from someone while using or showing a weapon.
  17. Extortion through violenceGetting money or favours from someone by threatening to hurt them.
  18. GenocideActs meant to destroy a national, ethnic, racial or religious group.
  19. Crimes against humanityWidespread, systematic attacks on civilians, such as murder or enslavement.
  20. FemicideKilling a woman or girl because she is female.
  21. ParricideKilling one’s own parent.
  22. Mass murderKilling many people in a single event.
  23. ArsonSetting fire to property on purpose.
  24. Acid attacksThrowing acid at someone to burn and disfigure them.

List by Parnian. Short definitions added by the coordinators to help with vocabulary.
