How to plan next year in times of war.

We can’t predict next month, let alone next year. So we’ll do it backwards: imagine the year has already happened, once as a failure and once as a success, and plan from there.

Before Friday

A note from the moderator

Hi everyone,

As you already know, our country is at war, and we are living in times of extreme uncertainty. The economy has been getting worse year by year, and most of us are in our 20s or 30s, the very years when we have the energy to build our lives and careers.

We could become victims and blame everything on the environment, which is not wrong, by the way. Or we could choose to take action and, even in the worst of times, build a fulfilling life for ourselves.

Some people think the only solution is to migrate to a better country, which is also a valid argument. But even as Iranians abroad, we still can’t make international transactions, and our currency has a low value, which makes the transition hard in the beginning. Being far away from our family, friends, and culture is hard, too. So migration is not a panacea. It might solve some problems, but it can create others, and it has its own hardships.

I believe each of us has a life purpose. No matter how messy our environment gets, we need to remember what that purpose is, put one foot in front of the other, and keep walking towards it.

I don’t have a one-size-fits-all answer to this question myself. I want to hear everyone’s thoughts and strategies, so that we can all make a better plan for the year ahead.

PouriaModerator

On the night

The plan, step by step

First we look ahead. Then we run the year twice in our heads: once where it goes wrong (a “pre-mortem”), and once where it goes right. We finish with one small step.

Your notes stay on this device only. Nobody else can see them.

1

Look ahead

  1. What are three things you hope to achieve in the next five years?

  2. Life purpose

    Are these goals aligned with your life purpose? Why or why not?

  3. Based on these, what is one target you want to hit by this time next year?

2

Imagine it’s a year from now, and you didn’t reach your target.

Be honest here. Naming the real risks now is how we avoid them.

  1. What did you do that led to this?

  2. What did you fail to do that led to this?

  3. Which relationships, knowledge, or skills were missing?

  4. Which inner beliefs or habits held you back?

3

Imagine it’s a year from now, and you did reach your target.

Now look back from the good ending. What got you there?

  1. What did you do that got you there?

  2. What did you stop doing or avoid that helped you get there?

  3. Which relationships, knowledge, and skills did you develop along the way?

  4. Which inner qualities or mindsets did you build?

4

One small step

  1. What is one small step you can take this week to move toward your target?

At the table

Questions from the future

Imagine it’s one year from now and you’ve reached the result you wanted. Looking back, think about these before Friday. We’ll talk about them together at the table.

  1. What did I consistently do that brought me to this result?
  2. Which inner strengths and potential did I invest in to get here? (My talents, skills, reading, training, and so on.)
  3. Which practical skills did I spend time and money learning, instead of dismissing them with psychological excuses?
  4. Where did I refuse to give up, no matter what happened and even when it was hard, and feel proud of myself for it?
  5. How did I gracefully move past things I didn’t like in my interactions with people, the distractions and drama, and stay focused on the bigger result that mattered to me?
  6. I added these things to my lifestyle, which made me healthier, more disciplined, and more purposeful: …
Whether we stay or leave

One foot in front of the other, and we keep walking. Together.
